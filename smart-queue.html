Job Fair Smart Queue

A live queue for every company booth at the SMU career fair. Students join from their phone and stay free to explore; organizers run every line from one dashboard.

Open the live app See the career fair project

Why I built it

When I restructured SMU's career fair, the biggest frustration for everyone was the queues. Students stood in one line for a long time without knowing how long it would take, and missed the other companies they came to meet. Recruiters faced crowds at some moments and empty booths at others.

Smart Queue replaces the physical line with a digital one. Students keep their place without standing in it, and the fair keeps moving.

For students

  1. Scan and joinScan the QR code at any company's booth. The app opens in the browser: no install and no account.
  2. Get a ticketA ticket number, your live position and an estimated wait time appear on your phone.
  3. Keep your place by emailAn email with a tracking link means you can check your place from anywhere, even after closing the tab.
  4. Explore freelyVisit other booths, get your CV reviewed or take a break while the line moves.
  5. Get calledWhen it's your turn, the app alerts you with a sound, a vibration and a notification.

For organizers

Staff run the fair from a PIN-protected dashboard.

  • Call the next studentOne tap calls the next person in line and alerts them on their phone.
  • Mark interviews doneFinished interviews and no-shows are marked in a tap, and the queue moves forward automatically.
  • See the whole fairLive stats for each company and each floor show where students are waiting.
  • Kept privateThe PIN keeps the dashboard in the hands of the fair's staff.

Built lean

I vibe coded Smart Queue to be as easy as possible to adopt: it runs in any phone browser, students don't need to sign up, and it costs nothing to run. The app is hosted for free on Netlify, with its data stored in Supabase.
